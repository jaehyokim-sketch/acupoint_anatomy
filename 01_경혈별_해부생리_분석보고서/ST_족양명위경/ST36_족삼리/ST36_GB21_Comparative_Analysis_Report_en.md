## 1. Overview and Purpose of the Comparison

This report compares **Zusanli (足三里, ST36)** of the Stomach Meridian of Foot-Yangming (足陽明胃經) with **Jianjing (肩井, GB21)** of the Gallbladder Meridian of Foot-Shaoyang (足少陽膽經) on the same basis (Framework v2.7, ARI v3.0). All figures and verdicts are carried over unchanged from the dual-scoring and adjudication results of the two individual reports; nothing is rescored in this report.

> **⚠ Safety warning first**: GB21 is a point at high risk of pneumothorax, lying directly above the cervical pleura and the apex of the lung. *Zhenjiu Shuxue Tongkao* (《針灸腧穴通考》) records that about 90% of needling-induced pneumothoraces arise at Jianjing (肩井), and the value in the needling-depth DB for *Donguibogam* (《東醫寶鑑》, quoting the *Tongren* 《銅人》) is `0` (needling prohibited). Avoid anteroinferior insertion and excessively deep perpendicular insertion.

## 3. Overall Findings and Clinical Guidance

1. **Zusanli (足三里, ST36)** is a point of probable (B) level whose anatomical substrate is the deep fibular nerve and anterior tibial artery/veins deep to tibialis anterior. The vagal–adrenal axis mechanism is concordant at B-Check 9/12 but rests on mouse evidence, and given the dependence on stimulus intensity (B3-16), the dual effect on gastric motility (papers.id 34) and the counter-evidence on point specificity (B4-04, B4-05), its application to humans must be stated with restraint. The spinal segments (L4–S1) do not match the stomach (T6–T9), so the gastrointestinal indications can only be explained through supraspinal pathways.
2. **Jianjing (肩井, GB21)** is a point of possible/hypothetical (C) level whose anatomical substrate is trapezius and the neurovascular structures beneath it. The reflex mechanism remains a hypothesis at B-Check 7/12. The most important clinical fact is the risk of pneumothorax: keep the needle shallow and do not direct it anteroinferiorly. Calculating the vertical component of a 45° oblique insertion alone does not guarantee safety.
3. For both points the ARI weights are provisional (0.40/0.35/0.25), and the grade did not change under the equal-weight sensitivity analysis.
4. **Two axes of safety**: the SI of 0 for GB21 is based on the "severity of the consequence" — the needling-prohibition literature and pneumothorax — while the SI of 75 for ST36 is based on the absence of needling-prohibition literature. Yet the same ultrasound study (papers.id 1896, Choi 2026, 39 subjects) reported a narrower effective-to-danger depth ratio (TI) for ST36 (1.09) than for GB21 (1.54). In other words, ST36 is a point where "a danger structure (the neurovascular bundle) lies close to the effective depth", and GB21 is a point where "the danger structure (the pleura) is relatively far away, but the consequences of injury are grave". Since the authors themselves describe the study as exploratory, the SI levels of the two points are not changed; the finding is stated alongside them in the clinical guidance.
