  • T2 safety: the standard 5–10 fen stays within the first dorsal interosseous (Layer 2) to adductor pollicis (Layer 3). The texts specify angle and posture restrictions, such as the 1959 Shanghai "針尖偏向第二掌骨方向……成80度角" ([通考] L701) and the 7th edition's "手呈半握拳狀". Verdict: the two textbooks agree on the superficial layer (dorsal venous network of hand, dorsal branches of the superficial radial nerve), the first dorsal interosseous and the radialis indicis artery, and all of these are confirmed as standard FMA entities. The differences lie in the deep layer. 1. Deep branch of ulnar nerve: listed only in the Kim Jaehyo textbook. A cadaveric study (papers.id 2292, 25 hands) confirmed that this nerve lies within the belly of the first dorsal interosseous, supporting Kim Jaehyo's entry. 2. Head of adductor pollicis: the Korean (transverse head) and English (oblique head) of the Kim Jaehyo textbook differ. At the level of the midpoint of the 2nd metacarpal (T2), the transverse head occupies the metacarpal-base side (arising from the shaft of the 3rd metacarpal) and the oblique head lies further proximally (toward the carpal bones) (B1-01), so one may interpret the transverse head as closer to T2 and the oblique head as closer to T1 (not measured).

Neither difference is a conflict; both are differences in the scope and level of description. This is the basis for the T2 $S_{clarity}$ of 75 (reconciled). Neither textbook describes T1 (the classical location).

8. Biomedical Reflex Mechanism Diagram & External Search Log

Mechanism summary (T2): Hegu is one of the points most used in research on acupuncture mechanisms. The confirmed evidence runs along three lines. 1. Human central responses: LI4 stimulation alone decreased fMRI signals in limbic and subcortical structures (papers.id 48, B4-01), activated the hypothalamus (PET, 19) and changed default mode network connectivity (1674, B4-02). 2. Autonomic: in humans, a transient increase in muscle sympathetic nerve activity (LI4 + LI11, 60), an increase in both sympathetic and parasympathetic activity (LI4 alone, 25), and parasympathetic predominance with reduced heart rate (LI4 + LU6, 17). In anaesthetised rats, sympathetic excitation and inhibition of gastric vagal activity (24). The direction differs between studies → the autonomic effect is restricted to a hypothesis. 3. Facial connection: in mice, tracer injected at LI4 travels to the C6 and C7 dorsal root ganglia, the spinal dorsal horn → thalamic VP and trigeminal ganglion → Sp5C → VP pathways converge in the thalamus, and LI4 electroacupuncture improved trigeminal neuralgia behaviour (2297). In humans, one study observed a hand–face connection via the palmomental reflex (2295). This supports, at the level of thalamic convergence, the hypothesis of a neural pathway for the classical saying "面口合谷收" (face and mouth are governed by Hegu) (animal).

B-Check 9/12 meets the concordance threshold (≥ 8/12) (T2). The conflicting autonomic directions are, however, kept as 'hypothesis' and the facial pathway as 'animal only'.

Final Conclusion

Hegu (合谷, LI04) exists at two sites. The classical point (T1) was a pulse-taking site just distal to the junction where the bases of the 1st and 2nd metacarpals meet, where the radial artery pulsation can be felt. The modern point (T2) was moved, from the 1964 textbook onward, to the midpoint of the radial side of the 2nd metacarpal. The two sites are about 2–3 cm apart (estimated), and neither can be judged superior to the other (ΔARI-Core 7.5).

  • The anatomical substrate of T2 is the first dorsal interosseous with the overlying superficial branch of the radial nerve and dorsal venous network of hand, and adductor pollicis in the deep layer. The two textbooks agree on the superficial layer, all structures are confirmed as standard FMA entities, and a human cadaveric study (2292) confirmed their arrangement. What keeps the grade at C is classical concordance: the modern site is not where most texts from the Han to the modern period point.
  • The reflex mechanism of T2 is concordant with B-Check 9/12. Several imaging studies agree that LI4 stimulation alone elicits limbic and hypothalamic responses in humans. The direction of the autonomic effect, however, differs between studies and remains a hypothesis. The hand–face pathway (thalamic VP convergence) explaining '面口合谷收' has been shown only in mice.
  • T1 has the strongest classical support, but the textbooks do not describe it and no mechanism study has specified this location.

Safety: 1. ⚠ Contraindicated in pregnancy: recorded consistently since 《銅人》. The record of miscarriage with the reinforcing method (補法) comes from combination with Sanyinjiao (三陰交, SP6) (Section 9). In a modern RCT (510), LI4 + SP6 electroacupuncture also raised oxytocin. 2. T1: this is the radial artery pulsation, so push the pulse aside and needle shallowly (3 fen). 3. T2: angle the needle toward the 2nd metacarpal (about 80°) with the hand in a half-clenched fist. Through-needling toward 勞宮 or 後溪 approaches the deep palmar arch and the deep branch of the ulnar nerve in the deep palmar layer. 4. Needle syncope (暈針): systemic reactions (needle syncope) are reported with strong stimulation, so the first treatment is safer in the supine position (《新編針灸學》).
