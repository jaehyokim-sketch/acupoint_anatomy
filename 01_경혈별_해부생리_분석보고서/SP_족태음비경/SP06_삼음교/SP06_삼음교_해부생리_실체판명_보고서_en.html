  • Multi-layered anatomical substrates, 'multi-target, multi-mode regulation within a single acupoint (一輸多點多模式調控)', and methodological prerequisites for sham-control design: The clinical data accumulated under Sanyinjiao (三陰交, Target 3) cannot be reduced to any single anatomical structure in the table above (e.g., either the superficial saphenous nerve branches or the deep tibial nerve alone). First, historically, clinical records from the original 8-cun 'Sanyinjiao' (Target 1≡2), the 3-cun 'Foot-Taiyin (足太陰)' point (Target 3), adjacent points such as Lougu (漏谷, SP7), and meridian-deduced indications were superimposed under the single name 'Sanyinjiao' (Huang Longxiang, 〈腧穴主治的演變〉, 《中國針灸》, 2001; see also papers.id 2511, 2535). Second, even within the current 3-cun three-dimensional zone (X·Y·Z), the superficial cutaneous nerves (FMA:75559), perivascular nerve plexus of the great saphenous vein (FMA:21376), and perivascular mast-cell/peptidergic network (papers.id 2558), the mechanoreceptors in the deep muscle layer (FMA:22542, 51071, 51099; papers.id 2321), and the deep neurovascular bundle (FMA:19035, 43895, 44332; papers.id 2602, 2603)—selectively recruited depending on needling depth, angle, and manipulation—correspond to distinct target organs and disease patterns, forming a structure of 'multi-target, multi-mode regulation within a single acupoint (一輸多點多模式調控)' (Huang Longxiang, 〈人體調控之門的探與測——基於現代解剖學和古典針灸學的視角〉, 《現代中醫臨床》, 2023: "同一輸穴中有不同的靶點…同一穴中的不同靶點調控的靶器官不同"; cf. the multi-tissue composite acupoint model in papers.id 2507). Consequently, when designing modern biomedical experiments or randomized controlled trials (RCTs) to test the point specificity of Sanyinjiao, researchers must first specify which anatomical layer (Layers 1–4) and structure constitute the intended stimulation target for a given indication, and ensure that 'non-acupoint shallow needling (非穴淺刺)' used as a sham control does not inadvertently stimulate the subcutaneous fascial layer—which in classical acupuncture is the standard analgesic technique known as 'fascial-plane needling (分刺法)' for general pain ('general Bi syndrome, 衆痹')—or accompanying perivascular autonomic nerve plexuses, thereby confounding the control group (Huang Longxiang, Xin Gudian Zhenjiuxue Dagang (《新古典針灸學大綱》), People's Medical Publishing House, 2022, Part 4, Section 2, Articles 183–184).

Historiographical and Biomedical Differentiation between 'Dysmenorrhoea Relief (痛經緩解)' and 'Contraindication in Pregnancy (孕婦禁針 / 催產)': Linking the two uterine-related attributes of Sanyinjiao (三陰交)—'relief of dysmenorrhoea' and 'caution/contraindication of needling during pregnancy (孕婦禁針)'—as a single, vague 'state-dependent bidirectional regulation (雙向調節)' of the uterus is neither historiographically nor biomedically valid. Instead, they must be understood as distinct strata formed in different historical contexts and governed by different stimulation conditions (anatomical layer, parameters, point combination, and uterine receptor state): 1. Historiographical and theoretical differentiation (Huang Longxiang's textual research): As demonstrated in Xin Gudian Zhenjiuxue Dagang (《新古典針灸學大綱》, 2022, Part 4, Section 1, Article 177: "雙向調節是機體固有的自穩機制而非針灸作用之功") and subsequent papers (〈人體調控之門的探與測〉, 《現代中醫臨床》, 2023; 〈指尖針尖下的人體形態結構探索發現及再發現〉, 《中國針灸》, 2025), bidirectional regulation is not a mystical property intrinsic to acupuncture, but an inherent negative-feedback homeostatic mechanism of the autonomic nervous system and vascular self-regulatory structures—and even when normalizing opposite pathological states of the same organ (e.g., constipation vs. diarrhoea, menorrhagia vs. hypomenorrhoea, overactive bladder vs. acute urinary retention papers.id 2552, hypertension vs. hypotension papers.id 2541), it depends on appropriate stimulation parameters (moreover, Article 169 〈助孕助產〉 of the same book cites Zhao Xi's (趙熙) finding that "胎前多針灸則分娩較易", noting that appropriate prenatal acupuncture preconditioning actually facilitates labour). In Sanyinjiao, dysmenorrhoea and menstrual-disorder relief accumulated as a therapeutic indication for promoting blood flow and regulating the lower jiao (通經行血) after the 3-cun 'Foot-Taiyin (足太陰)' point came to be called Sanyinjiao in the Sui–Tang period (papers.id 2511, 2535). By contrast, caution/contraindication in pregnancy originated from the Southern Dynasties physician Xu Wenbo's (徐文伯) emergency obstetric case of expelling a dead foetus (死胎不下) via "瀉足太陰，補手陽明" (where the Six-Dynasties '足太陰' point was likely located behind the medial malleolus per Yugui Zhenjing (《玉匱針經》)), which Song-dynasty Tongren Shuxue Zhenjiu Tujing (《銅人腧穴針灸圖經》) incorporated under the 3-cun Sanyinjiao as "瀉足三陰交，補手陽明合谷，應針而落。故妊娠不可刺", thereby generalizing a specific labour-inducing combination into a preventive contraindication (also confirmed as a primary contraindicated pair alongside Shimen CV5 in ancient-literature data mining, papers.id 2580). 2. Biomedical reflex differentiation (Layer A & Layer B): - Dysmenorrhoea relief pathway ($P_{single}$ and CV4 combination): Under conditions of prostaglandin ($\text{PGF}_{2\alpha}$) excess and ischaemic uterine spasm, stimulation primarily targeting Layer 3 (flexor digitorum longus FMA:51071, etc., L5–S2) downregulates local mechanosensitive Piezo1 channels, CGRP, and mast-cell degranulation and suppresses the uterine Piezo1/COX-2/$\text{PGF}_{2\alpha}$ pathway (papers.id 2321, SP6-alone 50 Hz EA), modulates uterine A20/NF-κB/NLRP3/GSDMD and miRNA-21-5p macrophage polarization anti-inflammatory pathways (papers.id 2041, 2322, CV4+SP6 combination), and engages supraspinal centres (hypothalamic PVN papers.id 2280, human amygdala connectivity papers.id 2314, and $^{18}\text{F-FDG}$ PET/CT thalamic, insular, amygdalar, hippocampal, ACC, and hypothalamic mammillary metabolic activation with significant VAS pain reduction during single-point SP6 perpendicular needling in primary dysmenorrhoea patients, papers.id 2520, 2521) to inhibit sympathetic hyperactivity and visceral pain. - Pregnancy caution / labour-induction pathway ($P_{comb}$, combined with Hegu LI4 and high-intensity gestational stimulation): Rather than an automatic response to SP6 alone, strong Deqi (得氣) stimulation reaching the Layer 4 deep tibial nerve trunk (FMA:19035, L4–S3; overlapping at S2 with the pelvic splanchnic nerve S2–S4) combined with Hegu (合谷, LI4; especially sequential needling of LI4 followed by SP6) elevates posterior pituitary oxytocin secretion (papers.id 510, 2024 human post-caesarean RCT, LI4+SP6) and sacral parasympathetic reflexes to promote phasic uterine contractions and cervical ripening. Empirically, in an early-pregnancy medical abortion RCT (papers.id 2516, $n=90$), LI4+SP6 EA (especially sequential LI4→SP6) relieved continuous cramping pain while converting spasms into rhythmic contractions that facilitated gestational-sac expulsion; in a term primipara labour-promotion and analgesia RCT (papers.id 2537, $n=150$), sequential needling of LI4 (20 min) followed 4 hours later by SP6+BL60 (30 min) achieved a 94.7% labour-promotion rate and 90.7% analgesia rate, shortening the first stage of labour; and in mid-gestation pregnant mice (days 9–11), bilateral single-point SP6 EA (30 min) combined with intramuscular oxytocin ($3.25\text{ U/kg}$) induced vaginal bleeding within 4 hours and reduced foetal, placental, and uterine weights, establishing a threatened-abortion model (papers.id 2551). Because such stimulation triggers strong phasic uterine contractions when myometrial oxytocin receptors are expressed or sensitized, it is established as a conditional safety precaution (SI 50) for pregnant women in general.

Final Conclusion

Sanyinjiao (三陰交) is a point whose name and substance have parted ways. The '三陰交' of Huangdi Mingtang Jing (《黃帝明堂經》) (Target 1≡2) lay 8 cun above the medial malleolus, in the depression below the tibia. The present Sanyinjiao (Target 3) is the Sui–Tang '足太陰' point (3 cun above the medial malleolus), which inherited this name (Tongkao name–reality study (名實考); summary (小結) of the Zhengzhi Tongjian). The anatomical substrate is established for the current Target 3 at the probable (B, ARI-Core 75.0 ± 2.5) level. Behind the posterior border of the medial surface of the tibia, the saphenous nerve branches and great saphenous vein (subcutaneous), soleus and flexor digitorum longus (and tibialis posterior), and deep to them the tibial nerve and posterior tibial artery and veins are confirmed by the two textbooks, the local anatomy in the Xuewei Tongjian, FMA, adult ultrasound measurement (papers.id 2602, depth $14.4\sim15.0\text{mm}$), and rat 9.4T MRI (papers.id 2603). A peripheral–central–target-organ (uterus, bladder, ovary) loop for stimulation of SP6 alone has been reported across animal models and human neuroimaging (papers.id 2520, 2521, 2314, 2519, 2589) and is concordant on the B-Check at 10/12. Target 1≡2 is the original owner of the name but, lacking textbook, measured and experimental evidence, remains at D·H.

Collation with the Zhongguo Zhenjiu Jingluo Tongjian (《中國針灸經絡通鑑》) supports the name–reality study from the channel side. The crossing of the foot-taiyin and foot-jueyin in 《靈樞·經脈》 lies 8 cun above the medial malleolus, and pre-Song channel charts (《醫心方》, 《產經》) put 三陰交 at 8 cun on the foot-jueyin side. The 3-cun site is the '足太陰' point moved there in the Sui–Tang period, and Yang Shangshan's (楊上善) Yin Linking Vessel origin theory and the Ming–Qing placement of a 'Sanyinjiao that three yin channels pass' are later meridian-theory interpretations. This collation does not change any rubric level. In Tri-Target terms, the classical text position and the palpated gap merge at 8 cun (Target 1≡2), branch from the WHO standard location (Target 3) by about 12–15 cm (estimated), and Target 3 alone is superior.

Limits on the scope of application: 1. The classical evidence for the opioid analgesic mechanism (Han's research group, B3-10) involves combined ST36+SP6 stimulation and is not used as evidence of an effect intrinsic to SP6. 4. SI 50: within the modern standard perpendicular range (adult ultrasound tibial-nerve depth $14.4\sim15.0\text{mm}$, papers.id 2602), contact with the tibial nerve and posterior tibial artery and veins is possible. Pregnant women are not needled. However, this contraindication derives from Xu Wenbo's (徐文伯) prescription for inducing delivery and is not recorded as 'Contraindicated for needling' ('0') in the needling-depth DB. Furthermore, 'dysmenorrhoea relief ($P_{single}$·$P_{comb}$)' and 'contraindication in pregnancy (labour induction, 催產·引產)' are not a single state-dependent bidirectional regulation of the uterus, but distinct historical and biomedical strata differing in origin (menstrual blood-regulating indication papers.id 2511, 2535 vs. generalisation of Xu Wenbo's labour-inducing case papers.id 2580) and stimulation conditions (Layer 3 Piezo1/COX-2/$\text{PGF}_{2\alpha}$, anti-inflammatory and central analgesic pathways papers.id 2321, 2520, 2521 vs. Layer 4 deep tibial-nerve stimulation combined with Hegu (合谷, LI4) elevating oxytocin secretion and phasic uterine contractions, papers.id 510, 2516, 2537, 2551) (Section 8). 5. Among the current indications, 'spleen disease with heavy body, abdominal distension and borborygmus, loose stools (脾病 身重·腹脹腸鳴·溏泄)' derives from Wang Tao's (王燾) misattribution (Tongkao). The 《明堂》 passage on '足下熱·脛痛' gives the indications of Target 1≡2 and is not used as evidence for the current point. 6. Prerequisites for biomedical verification and sham-control design: Because the clinical data of Sanyinjiao (Target 3) represent a composite of 'multi-target, multi-mode regulation within a single acupoint (一輸多點多模式調控)' across Layers 1–4, experimental and RCT designs must first specify the target anatomical layer and structure corresponding to the indication under study, and prevent 'non-acupoint shallow needling (非穴淺刺)' controls from inadvertently stimulating the subcutaneous fascial plane—the classical 'fascial-plane needling (分刺法)' for general pain (衆痹)—or perivascular nerve plexuses (Section 4; 《新古典針灸學大綱》 Articles 183–184).
