  2. Sui-Tang to Song-Jin-Yuan (隋唐·宋金元): - For the establishment of front-back and upper-lower near-distant point combinations (e.g. Shanglian + Shangjuxu, Yin Shaohai + Yang Shaohai), the corresponding prescriptions were not found in the three classics (author's judgement, not quantitatively verified).
  3. Ming-Qing period (明清時代): - [v2.7 correction] The 手上廉/足上廉 distinction began in Volume 191 of the Song-dynasty 《聖濟總錄》 but was not adopted by later texts, and the Ming-dynasty 《針灸聚英》 mixed the indications of ST37 into LI9 (《針灸腧穴通考》). The claim of "completion of the distinction and resolution of confusion" in the Ming-Qing period does not match the classics.
