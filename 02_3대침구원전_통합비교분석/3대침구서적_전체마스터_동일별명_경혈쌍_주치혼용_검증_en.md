## 🔬 Classification of Three Mechanisms Among Points Sharing the Same Alias

### 1. Type 1: Functional / Efficacy Alignment (Functional/Pathological Alignment)
- **Mechanism**: The interpretation that points that are physically separate share the same alias because their organ efficacies (kidney Yang (腎陽), Original Qi (元氣), calming the Spirit (安神), brightening the eyes (明目)) are the same (author's judgement — no evidence was found in the classics that efficacy alignment is the cause of alias sharing; caution regarding Rank 4 speculative interpretation). The aliases of the three representative pairs themselves are confirmed in the classics (石門 一名命門 · 攢竹 一名光明 · 大杼 本神).
- **Representative pairs**:
  - `CV05 Shimen (石門)` - `GV04 Mingmen (命門)` (shared alternative name: **命門**) ➔ warming kidney Yang (腎陽) and Original Qi; aligned indications of seminal emission, infertility and low-back pain.
  - `BL02 Cuanzhu (攢竹)` - `GB37 Guangming (光明)` (shared alternative name: **光明**) ➔ aligned indications of eye diseases, red eyes and brightening the eyes (明目).
  - `BL11 Dazhu (大杼)` - `GB13 Benshen (本神)` (shared alternative name: **本神**) ➔ aligned indications of brain/spinal-cord nerves, sequelae of stroke and calming the Spirit (安神).

### 2. Type 2: Anatomical / Muscle-Meridian Alignment (Anatomical/Kinetic Alignment)
- **Mechanism**: The anatomical-substrate hypothesis that, being located on anatomically symmetrical structures or adjacent joint-muscle structures of the elbow, knee, shoulder, face and pubis, the points overlap in their categories of indication for local pain and motor paralysis (ARI not scored, not quantitatively verified). ※ Of the four representative pairs below, only 橫骨–曲骨 (屈骨) is confirmed in the classics as sharing an alias; 少海 and 上關 are not confirmed, and 外丘 is limited to later literature.
- **Representative pairs**:
  - `HT03 Yin Shaohai (陰少海)` - `SI08 Yang Shaohai (陽少海)` (shared alternative name: **少海**) ➔ medial-lateral symmetry of the elbow joint; overlapping indication categories of elbow pain / tennis elbow (not quantitatively verified).
  - `ST32 Futu (伏兔)` - `GB36 Waiqiu (外丘)` (shared alternative name: **外丘**) ➔ lateral muscles of the thigh/calf; overlapping indication categories of lower-limb paralysis (not quantitatively verified).
  - `GB02 Tinghui (聽會)` - `GB03 Shangguan (上關)` (shared alternative name: **上關**) ➔ adjacent points in front of / above the auricle; overlapping indication categories of tinnitus, deafness and temporomandibular joint disorders (not quantitatively verified).
  - `KI11 Henggu (橫骨)` - `CV02 Qugu (曲骨)` (shared alternative name: **曲骨**) ➔ adjacent points on the upper border of the pubis; overlapping indication categories of the urogenital organs (not quantitatively verified).

### 3. Type 3: Textual Transmission and Lower He-Sea Compatibility (Textual Transmission & Lower Sea Compatibility)
- **Mechanism**: Transfer of indications through abbreviations in the ancient literature (*Neijing* (《內經》), *Jiayi Jing* (《甲乙經》)) (e.g. `巨虚上廉` ➔ `上廉`; whether a copying error or an original abbreviation is undetermined), or along the upper-lower course of the Yangming meridians (陽明經).
- **Representative pairs**:
  - `ST37 Shangjuxu (上巨虛)` - `LI09 Shanglian (上廉)` (shared alternative name: **上廉**) ➔ name sharing due to the ancient abbreviation of "巨虛上廉" is confirmed (上巨虛 alias '上廉'); compatibility of colitis/dysentery indications and point combination is not quantitatively verified (large-intestine disorders report: of 36 'Shanglian' rows, 18 explicitly ST37).
  - `ST39 Xiajuxu (下巨虛)` - `LI08 Xialian (下廉)` (shared alternative name: **下廉**) ➔ name sharing due to the ancient abbreviation of "巨虛下廉" is confirmed (下巨虛 alias '下廉'); compatibility of indications is not quantitatively verified.

## 🏛️ Historical Patterns of Indication Mixing and Fusion Through Point Combination by Era

1. **Pre-Qin to Wei, Jin and Northern-Southern Dynasties (先秦·魏晉南北朝)**:
   - Through abbreviations in the *Huangdi Neijing* (《黃帝內經》) and *Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing* (《針灸甲乙經》) (e.g. `Juxu Shanglian (巨虛上廉)` ➔ `Shanglian (上廉)`), the name overlaps with Shanglian of the Hand Yangming (the causal claim that this was the "origin" of indication mixing is the author's judgement).
2. **Sui-Tang to Song-Jin-Yuan (隋唐·宋金元)**:
   - For the establishment of front-back and upper-lower near-distant point combinations (e.g. `Shanglian + Shangjuxu`, `Yin Shaohai + Yang Shaohai`), the corresponding prescriptions were not found in the three classics (author's judgement, not quantitatively verified).
3. **Ming-Qing period (明清時代)**:
   - [v2.7 correction] The **手上廉/足上廉** distinction began in Volume 191 of the Song-dynasty 《聖濟總錄》 but was not adopted by later texts, and the Ming-dynasty 《針灸聚英》 mixed the indications of ST37 into LI9 (《針灸腧穴通考》). The claim of "completion of the distinction and resolution of confusion" in the Ming-Qing period does not match the classics.
4. **Modern and contemporary period (近代·現代)**:
   - Settled by the WHO international standard names and locations (B1-05). "Establishment of precise point-combination guidelines" is the author's judgement (no guideline literature cited).
