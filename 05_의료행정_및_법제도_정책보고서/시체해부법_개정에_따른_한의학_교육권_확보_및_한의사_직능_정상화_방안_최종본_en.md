# 【Policy Report】 Comprehensive Plan to Secure the Right to Korean Medicine Education and Normalise the Professional Scope of Korean Medicine Doctors Following the Amendment of the Corpse Dissection Act
**— Strategy for Building Infrastructure at Korean Medicine Colleges Nationwide and Advancing the Legal Framework Following the Full Enforcement of Act No. 21107, Presidential Decree No. 36725 and Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 01204 —**

## Executive Summary

> **[Key conclusions]**
> 1. **Institutional completion of the Act and the Enforcement Decree:** 
>    * Article 1 and Article 2 subparagraph 2 item (a) of the amended Act (No. 21107) expressly state **'medicine (including dentistry and Korean medicine)'** and **'medical colleges (including dental colleges and colleges of Korean medicine)'**, establishing in positive law the right of Korean medicine colleges to regular anatomy dissection practice and the authority of their faculty to dissect and supervise dissection directly.
>    * Presidential Decree No. 36725, in force from 29 September 2026, newly created a permit system for **'corpse-providing institutions for educational purposes'** (Decree Article 3, Annex 1) and provided a special rule for the shared use of a university's existing facilities (Annex 1, Remark 1), opening the way for Korean medicine colleges to build independent cadaver-supply infrastructure.
> 2. **Decisive achievements of the Enforcement Rule of 12 October 2026 (Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 01204):**
>    * **'Korean Medicine Doctors (韓醫師)' expressly written into law as members of the Corpse Dissection Review Committee (Rule Article 1-2(2)):** 
>      **"Physicians (including dentists and Korean Medicine Doctors)"** are expressly stipulated as qualified members of dissection review committees. This secures a firm basis in positive law for appointing Korean Medicine Doctors as formal review members not only at Korean medicine colleges nationwide but also on the dissection review committees of general hospitals.
>    * **Finalisation of establishment-permit and management procedures (Rule Articles 4 to 7-2):**
>      The administrative and licensing framework has been finalised, including the application for permission as a 'corpse-providing institution for education' (Form No. 2), the duty of review when providing corpses and reporting at least four times a year (Rule Article 6(6)), and the annual status report through the KDCA information system by 31 December each year (Rule Article 7-2).
> 3. **Remaining tasks for the Korean Medicine Doctor's professional scope and a two-track strategy:**
>    * Article 2 subparagraph 1 of the Act still names only 'physicians (including dentists)' as persons entitled to dissect independently, so the independent cadaver-research right of clinical Korean Medicine Doctors needs to be supplemented.
>    * Using as leverage the legal logic by which Rule Article 1-2(2) names 'Korean Medicine Doctors' as qualified review members, the profession should vigorously pursue **[Stage 1: use of current law through appointment as adjunct faculty / clinical researchers of Korean medicine colleges]** and **[Stage 2: a legislative petition to the National Assembly to amend the Act so that Article 2 subparagraph 1 includes 'Korean Medicine Doctors']**.

## Chapter 1. Background and the Environment of the Latest Statutory Amendments

### 1.1 Historical significance of the amendment of the corpse dissection statutes
Under the former corpse dissection statutes, the definitions of 'medicine' and 'medical college' did not state whether Korean medicine and colleges of Korean medicine were included, so cadaver practice and the supply of donated bodies at Korean medicine colleges depended on unstable authoritative interpretations. 

Act No. 21107, in force from 12 May 2026, expressly states **'medicine (including dentistry and Korean medicine)'** in Article 1 (Purpose) and unifies the statutory term as **'medical colleges (including dental colleges and colleges of Korean medicine)'** in Article 2 subparagraph 2 item (a), thereby firmly recognising the legal status of Korean medicine education and research within the national health-care legal system.

### 1.2 Completion of the three-tier statutory framework with the promulgation of the latest Enforcement Rule on 12 October 2026
* **12 May 2026**: Full enforcement of Act No. 21107
* **29 Sep 2026**: Promulgation and enforcement of Presidential Decree No. 36725 (Enforcement Decree) (standards for corpse-providing institutions for educational purposes and special rule on shared use of facilities newly created)
* **12 Oct 2026**: **Promulgation and enforcement of the wholly amended Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 01204 (Enforcement Rule)**
  * Composition, operation and disqualification of the Corpse Dissection Review Committee (Articles 1-2 to 1-4) finalised: **'Korean Medicine Doctors' expressly written into the qualifications of review members**
  * Establishment permit for corpse-providing institutions for education (Article 4, Form No. 2) and standards for administrative dispositions (Article 5, Annex) completed
  * Provision procedures, calculation of expenses, reporting to the review committee four times a year (Article 6) and annual reporting of collection and use (Article 7-2) made mandatory

This fully resolves the uncertainty of the pre-announced 'draft' stage, and the three-tier positive-law framework (Act – Enforcement Decree – Enforcement Rule) is completely in place.

### 2.2 Legal significance of Enforcement Rule Article 1-2(2): statutory designation of Korean Medicine Doctor members
The provision that deserves the most attention in the Enforcement Rule finalised on 12 October is **Article 1-2(2)**:
> *"Members of a dissection review committee shall be appointed by the head of a medical college (including dental colleges and colleges of Korean medicine) or the head of a general hospital from among **physicians (including dentists and Korean Medicine Doctors)** or relevant experts with extensive knowledge and experience in education and research related to corpse dissection."*

* **Institutional legitimacy secured:** **'Korean Medicine Doctor'** is now stated as a legal term in the qualifications for membership of the highest national review body that ultimately reviews and decides on the ethics and validity of dissection.
* **Leverage for amending Act Article 2 subparagraph 1:** Whereas Article 2 subparagraph 1 of the parent Act (persons who dissect) names only 'physicians (including dentists)', Rule Article 1-2(2) already provides 'physicians (including dentists and Korean Medicine Doctors)'; so, if only to bring the parent Act and the subordinate statutes into systematic consistency, **the case for an amendment including Korean Medicine Doctors in Act Article 2 subparagraph 1 is fully proven in legal terms**.

## Chapter 3. Four Core Strategies for Securing the Right to Korean Medicine Education

### 3.1 [Strategy 1] Applications for KDCA authorisation of the 12 Korean medicine colleges (and graduate school) nationwide as 'corpse-providing institutions for education'
* **Use of the statutory form:** Apply for authorisation to the KDCA Division of Life Science Research Management using **Enforcement Rule [Form No. 2] 'Application for an establishment permit for a corpse-providing institution for education'**, finalised on 12 October.
* **Application of the [Annex 1] special rule on shared use of facilities:** Remodel the existing anatomy laboratory in the university to install BL2 (Biosafety Level 2) negative-pressure air-handling equipment, an infection-screening laboratory, a refrigerated mortuary at or below 4 °C and lockable shelving, meeting the permit standards at minimum cost.
* **Statutory staffing of 3 persons:** 
  1. Head (1 person): full-time professor of anatomy
  2. Manager (1 person): technician dedicated to embalming/fixation and management of bodies
  3. Counsellor/administrator (1 person): administrator dedicated to receiving donations, counselling bereaved families and computerised reporting to the information system

### 3.2 [Strategy 2] Standard regulations for each university's 'Corpse Dissection Review Committee' and appointment of Korean Medicine Doctor members
In accordance with Enforcement Rule Articles 1-2 and 1-3, establish and operate a review committee at each Korean medicine college immediately:
* **Composition (at least 5 members):**
  * Internal Korean Medicine Doctor professors and basic-medicine professors (chair elected by the members)
  * At least 2 holders of a master's or doctoral degree in anatomy, pathology or forensic medicine (mandatory)
  * At least 1 external member not employed by the university (mandatory)
* **Operating process (Rule Article 1-3, Article 6(6)):**
  * Ordinary regular practice in the main curriculum exempt from review (Act Article 9(2)), guaranteeing autonomy
  * Review of agenda items for dissection researching human structure, and collection of **regular reports on the status of corpse provision at least 4 times a year**
  * Use of written resolutions for minor matters to secure administrative speed

### 3.3 [Strategy 3] Launch of an independent Korean medicine 'public network for body-donation registration and honouring of bereaved families'
* **Introduction of a standard integrated consent form:** Distribute Enforcement Rule [Form No. 1] 'Consent to corpse dissection, preservation, research and provision' as the standard form at the intake desks of all 12 universities.
* **Public honouring of donors (Rule Article 9):** In connection with support programmes of the Ministry of Health and Welfare, KDCA and local governments (Rule Article 9), make public the system of regular memorial services for donors, a hall of honour, psychological counselling for bereaved families and funeral support.
* **Joint campaign with the Association of Korean Medicine:** Led by Korean Medicine Doctor members and alumni of Korean medicine colleges, conduct a 'body-donation pledge movement for the development of Korean medicine education after death' to build a stable cadaver-supply pool.

### 3.4 [Strategy 4] Innovation of advanced anatomy curricula to strengthen clinical competence
* **Ultrasound-guided pharmacopuncture and needle-knife (刀針) cadaver practice:** In step with the Supreme Court ultrasound ruling, keep ultrasound devices permanently in the dissection room and make real-time needling training at nerve-entrapment (Entrapment) sites a regular part of the curriculum.
* **Three-dimensional anatomical verification of 14 acupoints at Layers 1–5:** Run an advanced practical course in which the layer-by-layer nerve, vessel and fascial structures of the acupoints are dissected and observed directly.
* **Link to KASIE (Korean Institute of Korean Medicine Education and Evaluation) accreditation:** Reflect 'whether the institution is authorised as a corpse-providing institution' and 'hours of cadaver practice per student' in the core accreditation indicators of the Korean Institute of Korean Medicine Education and Evaluation.

## Chapter 4. Roadmap for Institutional Reform to Normalise the Professional Scope of Korean Medicine Doctors (Right to Clinical Research Dissection)

### 4.1 [Analysis of the core issue] Legality of corpse dissection by ordinary clinical Korean Medicine Doctors (independent dissection impossible vs. supervised dissection lawful)

On the basis of the recently amended Act No. 21107 and the Enforcement Rule of 12 October 2026 (Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 01204), the positive-law assessment of whether ordinary clinical Korean Medicine Doctors working in private practice and training hospitals may dissect corpses is clearly divided as follows.

#### ■ Comparison of legality by statutory provision and practical standards

| Item | Independent, autonomous corpse dissection | Corpse dissection under university-linked supervision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Assessment of legality** | ❌ **Impossible under positive law (unlawful)** | ⭕ **Fully lawful (lawful)** |
| **Applicable statutory provision** | **Act Article 2 subparagraph 1** | **Act Article 2 subparagraph 2 item (a)** |
| **Statutory requirements** | A **"physician (including dentists)"** with considerable knowledge and experience of corpse dissection who holds the qualifications prescribed by Presidential Decree and has passed review by the review committee | Where a person teaching anatomy etc. at a **"medical college (including colleges of Korean medicine)"** dissects directly or has **"a person or student who has majored in medicine"** **dissect under his or her supervision** |
| **Limits for Korean Medicine Doctors** | Because **'Korean Medicine Doctors' are expressly excluded** by the wording of the Act, independent dissection authority cannot be obtained even by applying for review-committee approval (violation punishable by imprisonment or a fine under Act Article 20) | Ordinary Korean Medicine Doctors clearly fall under **'a person who has majored in Korean medicine (medicine)'** in law, so practice under the supervision of the chair of the anatomy department of a Korean medicine college is 100% lawful |
| **Practical solution** | • Independent cadaver workshops in private practice are absolutely impossible under current law<br>• **[Legislative task]** Amendment adding 'Korean Medicine Doctors' to Act Article 2 subparagraph 1 is essential | • Establish a 'clinical neuroanatomy training course' run by Korean medicine colleges<br>• Appoint participating clinical Korean Medicine Doctors as adjunct faculty / clinical researchers so that they can take part |

#### ■ A serious legal contradiction in the current legal system
* **Review authority (Enforcement Rule Article 1-2(2)):** Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 01204 expressly provides **"physicians (including dentists and Korean Medicine Doctors)"** as qualified members of the 'Corpse Dissection Review Committee', the highest national review body that reviews and decides on the ethics and validity of dissection.
* **Dissection authority (Act Article 2 subparagraph 1):** By contrast, Article 2 subparagraph 1 of the parent Act names only 'physicians (including dentists)' as persons who dissect and omits Korean Medicine Doctors.
* **Legal conclusion:** This is a clear legislative deficiency and systemic contradiction in which **"a Korean Medicine Doctor may, as a formal member, review and approve the dissection research of others, yet cannot personally dissect independently"**. Amending Act Article 2 subparagraph 1 is therefore a natural measure of systemic normalisation that corrects this contradiction.

### 4.2 [Short-term strategy] Legalising cadaver training for clinical Korean Medicine Doctors within the bounds of current law
Even before the Act is amended, current law will be actively used so that clinical Korean Medicine Doctors in private practice and training hospitals can conduct needle-knife and pharmacopuncture practice using cadavers lawfully and stably:
1. **Model of appointment as adjunct faculty / clinical researchers of Korean medicine colleges (Act Article 2 subparagraph 2 item (a)):**
   * Establish a 'master course in clinical applied anatomy' overseen by the chair of anatomy of a Korean medicine college, and appoint participating clinical Korean Medicine Doctors as adjunct lecturers or clinical researchers of the university, so that they dissect and practise on cadavers lawfully under the chair's supervision.
2. **Translational-medicine research linked with public and university-hospital research institutes (Act Article 9(1), Rule Article 6):**
   * Use cadavers in the capacity of translational-medicine researchers after review by the Institutional Review Board (IRB) and dissection review committee of the National Medical Center, the National Rehabilitation Center or the research institutes of hospitals affiliated with Korean medicine colleges.

### 4.3 [Medium- to long-term strategy] Legislative petition to the National Assembly to amend Article 2 subparagraph 1 of the Corpse Dissection Act

#### ■ Comparison table of current and amended provisions: draft amendment to Article 2 subparagraph 1 of the Corpse Dissection Act
| Current (Act No. 21107) | Amendment (draft legislative petition) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Article 2 (Dissection of corpses)** A corpse may be dissected in any of the following cases:<br>1. Where it is dissected by a **physician (including dentists; hereinafter the same applies)** who has considerable knowledge and experience of corpse dissection, holds the qualifications prescribed by Presidential Decree and has undergone review by the Corpse Dissection Review Committee under Article 2-2 | **Article 2 (Dissection of corpses)** -----------------------------------------------------------------------.<br>1. --------------------------------------------------- **physician (including dentists and Korean Medicine Doctors; hereinafter the same applies)**------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

#### ■ Three decisive arguments for the legislative amendment:
1. **Harmony with Enforcement Rule Article 1-2(2) (systemic consistency):**
   * Because Article 1-2(2) of Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 01204 already provides **"physicians (including dentists and Korean Medicine Doctors)"** as qualified members of the review committee, including Korean Medicine Doctors among the persons who dissect in Article 2 subparagraph 1 of the parent Act is the natural consequence in terms of the legal system.
2. **Consistency with the purpose clause in Act Article 1:**
   * Completes, in the substantive provisions, the intent of the purpose clause declared in Act Article 1 as 'medicine (including dentistry and Korean medicine)'.
3. **Continuation of the legal reasoning of the Supreme Court en banc decisions (ultrasound, electroencephalograph, etc.):**
   * Guaranteeing clinical Korean Medicine Doctors' right to research human structure is essential to ensure the safety of their use of scientific-technological diagnostic devices and of invasive treatments (pharmacopuncture, needle-knife).

## Chapter 5. Administrative Support System and Comprehensive Action Plan (Action Plan)

### 5.1 Linkage with the KDCA integrated body information system and statutory annual reporting (Rule Article 7-2)
Under Enforcement Rule Article 7-2, the head of each Korean medicine college must report, **by 31 December each year** through the KDCA integrated information system, the quantity carried over from the previous year and the quantities collected, used, provided and disposed of in the current year. Because failure to report incurs an administrative fine of up to KRW 5 million (Decree Annex 2), a dedicated administrative process will be kept in operation at all times.

### 5.3 Proposal for national-treasury financial support from the government (Ministry of Health and Welfare / KDCA)
* **Subsidy for negative-pressure and air-handling remodelling of BL2 practice laboratories at the 12 Korean medicine colleges:** KRW 300–500 million per college (total KRW 4 billion in national subsidies)
* **National subsidy for the personnel costs of dedicated body-management technicians and counselling staff:** KRW 50 million per college per year

### 5.4 Proposal for the immediate launch of a pan-Korean-medicine joint task force
We strongly recommend the immediate launch of a **"Special Committee for Securing the Right to Anatomy Education in Korean Medicine and Normalising the Professional Scope" (provisional name)**, with the joint participation of the Association of Korean Medicine, the Korean Council of Colleges and Graduate Schools of Korean Medicine, the Society of Korean Medicine and the Korean Institute of Korean Medicine Education and Evaluation, to unify the official channel of consultation with the KDCA Division of Life Science Research Management and the National Assembly Health and Welfare Committee.
